# Privacy

LiquidMouse works entirely on your Mac. It makes no network connections, has no accounts and collects nothing.

What it reads while it runs:

- **Mouse and keyboard**: the pointer position and buttons, to draw the cursor, and whether a key was pressed, to
  hide while you type. Never which keys.
- **The system cursor and the app in front**: to pick the shape to draw and to step aside for games, full-screen
  apps and the apps you choose.
- **With the Accessibility permission only**: what kind of element is under the pointer (a button, a link). Never
  its text or content. You can revoke the permission at any time and LiquidMouse keeps working without it.

Your settings stay in the app's preferences. LiquidMouse also writes to the macOS system log, which can include
pointer positions and names of apps in front; the log stays on your Mac.

*In italiano: LiquidMouse funziona solo sul tuo Mac, non si collega a internet e non raccoglie nulla. Con il
permesso Accessibilità legge solo il tipo di elemento sotto il puntatore, mai il suo contenuto.*
